# Indian Cooking App

## Vision
A global Indian cooking app that helps users discover and prepare Indian dishes.

## Main Features

### 1. Cook With What I Have
Users select ingredients they already have.
The app suggests Indian dishes they can make.
This feature does NOT ask how many people they are cooking for.

### 2. Choose a Dish
Users select:
- Vegetarian
- Non-Vegetarian
- State/Region
- City/Regional cuisine
- Dish

The app then asks how many people they are cooking for and automatically adjusts ingredient quantities.

### 3. Explore India
Users can explore Indian cuisine by state and region.

Initial target:
- 20 vegetarian dishes per state
- 20 non-vegetarian dishes per state

### 4. Cooking Mode
Recipes are presented step by step with:
- Ingredients
- Quantities
- Cooking instructions
- Cooking time
- Timers
- Spice level

### 5. Languages
Initial languages:
- English
- Hindi
- Urdu
- Tamil
- Telugu

More languages can be added later.

### 6. Global Access
The app is intended for users worldwide.

Ingredients will use generic descriptions rather than brand recommendations.

Example:
- Basmati rice
- Plain yoghurt
- Salt

The app will not recommend specific supermarket or food brands.

## Important Rule
Dish names and recipes must be verified for regional authenticity before publication.
